One dad joke, at most 200 characters, for the [Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5) on make.fun.

The entry program (`main.ts`) writes a single dad joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My dad changes the air filter every month. It's the only filter he has — the kids get the unfiltered version.

A father keeps the house air filtered and admits it is the only filter he has — his children get him unfiltered. The pun lands on the last two words, reads aloud like a story told across the dinner table, and stays inside the dad joke tradition. The mechanism (an air filter as the one filter a blunt dad owns) was checked against published dad jokes and pun lists before it was written: 25 candidate mechanisms were tested, 21 discarded as already known, and this one was not found anywhere.
